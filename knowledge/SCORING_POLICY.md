# SCORING_POLICY.md

## Goal
Estimate IELTS Speaking performance consistently and conservatively for practice use.

## Four criteria
1. Fluency and Coherence (FC)
2. Lexical Resource (LR)
3. Grammatical Range and Accuracy (GRA)
4. Pronunciation (PRON)

## Evidence rules
- FC: transcript organization + timing/pauses + disfluency features
- LR: transcript vocabulary range, precision, collocation, paraphrase
- GRA: transcript sentence patterns, complexity, correctness
- PRON: audio-derived signals first, transcript only as support

## Conservative scoring
- If evidence for the higher band is incomplete, choose the lower band.
- Do not over-reward a few advanced words if the overall spoken control is weaker.
- Do not over-reward one long answer if coherence is weak or repetitive.

## Part-specific weighting notes
### Part 1
- short natural answers
- relevance
- ability to extend briefly
- not too much pressure for abstract reasoning

### Part 2
- long-turn development
- organization over 1–2 minutes
- cue-card coverage
- sustained coherence without examiner support

### Part 3
- abstract reasoning
- opinion + justification
- comparison, cause/effect, hedging, concession
- ability to discuss at length coherently

## Pronunciation caution
- Low ASR confidence does not automatically equal pronunciation failure.
- Combine ASR confidence with pause timing, repetition, and phonological features if available.

## Feedback principle
Give:
- one main reason for the score ceiling
- one next action most likely to improve the next half-band
