# STYLE_SPOKEN_REGISTER.md

## Purpose
Ensure all generated speaking answers sound like real spoken English.

## Rules
- Contractions are natural: I'm, I've, I'd, it's, that's, don't, can't.
- Avoid semicolons.
- Prefer short-to-medium spoken clauses.
- Avoid dense academic prose.
- Prefer natural discourse markers:
  - honestly
  - to be fair
  - I’d say
  - that said
  - at the same time
  - for example
  - on the other hand
  - having said that

## Band-sensitive language
### Band 5–6
- mostly clear, not overly sophisticated
- some linking words
- simple extensions

### Band 6.5–7
- flexible connectives
- some less common but natural vocabulary
- occasional idiomatic phrasing
- clearer justification and examples

### Band 7.5–8+
- precise word choice
- strong control of paraphrase
- smooth topic development
- advanced but still natural spoken phrasing

## Avoid
- essay openings like "In contemporary society"
- overly formal nouns in casual topics
- forced idioms
- unnatural passive voice
- sentences too long to say comfortably
