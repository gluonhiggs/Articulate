# IELTS_SPEAKING_DESCRIPTORS.md

This file is a practical restatement of the public IELTS Speaking band descriptors for app behavior.
It is not a replacement for the official descriptors.

## Criteria
- Fluency and Coherence
- Lexical Resource
- Grammatical Range and Accuracy
- Pronunciation

## Practical band interpretation

### Band 5
- noticeable hesitation and limited expansion
- basic vocabulary with frequent imprecision
- grammar errors are common
- pronunciation may reduce ease of understanding at times

### Band 6
- can speak at some length but may lose coherence
- enough vocabulary to discuss common topics
- mix of simple and some complex structures
- pronunciation generally understandable, though not consistently controlled

### Band 7
- can speak at length without obvious strain
- discourse markers used with some flexibility
- some less common vocabulary and paraphrase
- frequent error-free sentences, though mistakes remain
- pronunciation shows many positive features of band 6 and some of band 8

### Band 8
- fluent with only occasional language-search hesitation
- wide vocabulary used flexibly
- wide range of structures, majority error-free
- easy to understand throughout

## Part reminders
### Part 1
Natural, brief, relevant, extend answers a bit.

### Part 2
Organize and sustain a long turn.
Cover the cue card.
Do not just list points.

### Part 3
Explain, compare, justify, generalize carefully, and hedge when appropriate.
